RegIntel

                
Retrieval-Augmented AI for Regulatory Compliance

              

            

            

              
📋 Overview

              
A domain-specialized RAG system that answers questions about SEC filings and
                CFR regulatory rules using evidence-based retrieval and large language models.

Polymer Energy Prediction

                
Funded ML System for Materials Discovery

              

            

            

              
📋 Overview

              
A Rutgers-funded pipeline predicting polymer energy states from 2,916 CIF
                structures, helping identify low-energy configurations for layer formation.

                features and nearest-neighbor interactions fed stacked ensembles (RF, XGBoost, LightGBM, MLP), raising
                performance from 73% to 80% MCC, with a React dashboard and 3D structure viewer for researchers.
